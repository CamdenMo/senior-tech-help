Founder NameFounder, Community Tech Care

There’s a human side to every technology question. Sometimes it’s a photo someone wants to share. A call they don’t want to miss. Or a device they’d simply like to understand a little better.

Community Tech Care is built around making those everyday moments easier, with friendly, personal technology assistance in senior living communities.

Why this work matters

Getting help should feel comfortable. Asking a question should never feel like an interruption.

Our founder enjoys helping people feel more at ease with technology. The approach begins with listening: what would this person like to do, and what’s getting in the way?

By bringing support into residential communities, we make space for practical learning in a familiar setting. We work with community management to offer a thoughtful resident amenity, built around real questions and individual needs.

What residents can expect

Support with respect,
every step of the way.

Patience, always.

We work at each person’s pace, welcome repeated questions, and allow time to practice. There’s no rush to keep up.

Clarity over jargon.

Familiar words, simple steps, and practical examples help residents understand what they’re doing and why.

Help that empowers.

Residents stay involved and in control. We guide, encourage, and respect personal choices, privacy, and comfort levels.
